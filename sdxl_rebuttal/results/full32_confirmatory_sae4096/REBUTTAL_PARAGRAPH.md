We added an end-to-end experiment on the frozen SDXL-base text-conditioned latent U-Net, using 32 fixed real CelebA-HQ images, 50-step DDIM inversion, and identical prompts, latents, layers, and timesteps across methods. For Smiling, at matched target strength (four-classifier signed change 0.283 for CASL versus 0.276 for a matched dense H-space intervention), CASL reduced four-classifier off-target change from 0.109 to 0.078 and LPIPS from 0.113 to 0.060, while improving ArcFace identity from 0.407 to 0.527 and mean four-classifier EPR from 2.354 to 4.016. Paired-bootstrap 95% intervals exclude zero for all four specificity/preservation improvements. Results are concept-dependent: Young is mixed at matched strength and Blond Hair did not confirm at 32 images. We therefore claim evidence that CASL can transfer to a large pretrained text-conditioned latent U-Net without backbone fine-tuning, not universal superiority, fully zero-shot domain generalization, or empirical transfer to DiT or rectified-flow architectures.
